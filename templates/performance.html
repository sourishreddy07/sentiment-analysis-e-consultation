ML Pipeline Architecture & Hyperparameters

1. Preprocessing Pipeline

  • NLTK Tokenizer: Word punctuation tokenization
  • Normalization: Lowercase standardizing & symbol cleanup
  • Stopwords: English stopword removal
  • Lemmatization: WordNet lemmatizer with noun/verb fallback

2. Feature Extraction

  • Algorithm: TF-IDF Vectorizer
  • N-Gram Range: (1, 2) Unigrams & Bigrams
  • Max Vocabulary: 5,000 features
  • Sublinear TF: Logarithmic term scaling enabled

3. Calibration & Baseline

  • Primary Classifier: LinearSVC with dual='auto'
  • Calibration: CalibratedClassifierCV for confidence scores
  • Lexicon Baseline: VADER Compound Polarity Index
  • Persistence: Joblib model and vectorizer serialization
